What EquiMatch is

EquiMatch helps horse owners find and message equine professionals in the United States. Basic membership is free. The app is for ages 13 and up.

Account help

Use the in-app password recovery flow if you cannot sign in. For account access issues, deletion requests, or safety concerns, email us from the address on your EquiMatch account when possible.

Reporting and safety

You can report profiles and reviews from inside the app. You can also email Equimatchsupport@gmail.com with the profile, conversation, or review involved and a short description of the issue.

Privacy and data deletion

Read our Privacy Policy. To request account deletion or access to your personal data, email Equimatchsupport@gmail.com. We complete verified privacy and deletion requests within 30 days unless legally prevented.
