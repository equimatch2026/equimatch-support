EquiMatch Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026 · Operator: Josie Moisio

This policy describes how EquiMatch (“we”, “us”) handles personal information in the EquiMatch mobile app for horse owners and equine professionals in the United States. Contact: Equimatchsupport@gmail.com.

Information we collect

Depending on how you use EquiMatch, we may collect:

  • Account details: name, email address, password, role (horse owner or equine professional), age confirmation (13+), and community-agreement acceptance date/version.
  • Profile content: bio, photos, gallery images, professional business details (such as business name, experience, pricing signals, and service category), and for owners, horse names, details, and photos when you add them.
  • Location: precise location only when you allow it, used to help find nearby professionals inside EquiMatch. Location is not used for advertising or sold to data brokers.
  • Messages and reviews: conversation content, notifications related to messages, and reviews you submit or receive.
  • Device and technical data: device identifiers used for push notifications and app operation; diagnostics and performance data that may be collected by the app platform or included SDKs (such as maps-related components).
  • Support communications: emails and reports you send to support.

How we use information

  • Provide account access, profiles, search/discovery, messaging, saved professionals, and reviews
  • Show nearby professionals when you enable location
  • Enforce community terms, age requirements, blocking, suspension, and moderation
  • Respond to support, privacy, and deletion requests
  • Maintain security, prevent abuse, and operate the service
  • Comply with law

We do not sell personal information. We do not use your location for third-party advertising.

Who can see what

  • Professionals appear in the in-app directory for signed-in members according to app privacy rules.
  • Horse-owner profiles and horse details are visible to the owner and to professionals the owner has contacted, not to the full public directory.
  • Messages are limited to conversation participants.
  • Reviews appear according to moderation/publication rules.
  • Anonymous visitors do not receive member profile photo access under current app privacy configuration.

Sharing

We share information with service providers that help us run EquiMatch, including our app platform (Bubble) and infrastructure/services they rely on (for example hosting, file storage, maps, and diagnostics). Those providers process data on our behalf to operate the app. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect users and the service.

Retention and deletion

We keep account and related records while your account is active and as needed for safety, legal, and operational purposes.

You may request account deletion by emailing Equimatchsupport@gmail.com. Verified deletion and privacy requests are completed within 30 days unless legally prevented.

Current deletion scope: EquiMatch supports account deletion and removes associated profile access. Automatic permanent erasure of every uploaded file, replacement photo, gallery file, related conversation/message/review/notification/device record is still being finalized. Until that cleanup is fully verified, some uploaded files or associated records may be retained after reference removal. We will update this section when full erasure coverage is verified.

Children

EquiMatch is for users age 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Signup requires age confirmation and community-agreement acceptance.

Your choices

  • Edit or clear profile content in the app
  • Decline location permission (you can still use search without nearby results)
  • Block conversations and report profiles/reviews
  • Request access, correction, or deletion by emailing support

Security

We use account authentication, privacy rules, and operational controls to protect data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above will change when we do. Material updates may also be noted in the app or by email when appropriate.
